## 1. Overview & Classification Schema

This register catalogs the concrete technical gaps identified during the WP-01 literature review and archive audit. Gaps are categorized into three operational tiers:
1. **MVP-Critical (Release 1):** Mandatory technical requirements that must be resolved through experiments or implementation before the functional baseline MVP can be completed.
2. **Version 1.0 (Release 2):** Optimizations and advanced capabilities necessary for a robust, resume-worthy, production-grade system.
3. **Research / Experimental:** Exploratory architectures and complex algorithms to be investigated in isolated spikes without blocking releases.
